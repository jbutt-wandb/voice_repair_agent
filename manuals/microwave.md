# WaveMaster MW-450 Countertop Microwave Oven

## Product Overview
900 W countertop microwave with turntable, mechanical door latch with triple interlock
switches, mica waveguide cover inside the cavity, and a high-voltage circuit (transformer,
capacitor, magnetron) that **retains a lethal charge even when unplugged**.

## Safe Troubleshooting — steps you MAY guide the customer through
- **Dead / no display**: check the socket with another appliance; check the household fuse box;
  try unplugging for 60 seconds to reset the control board.
- **Runs but doesn't heat (first checks only)**: confirm the power level isn't set low, the
  door closes cleanly with no packaging debris in the latch, and a test cup of water on high
  for 60 seconds stays cold — if it stays cold, stop there: this requires an engineer.
- **Turntable not rotating**: remove the glass tray and roller ring, wash them, clear food
  debris from the drive coupling, and reseat both correctly.
- **Sparking inside**: stop using the oven; check for metal objects, foil, or metallic-trim
  crockery; inspect the mica waveguide cover for burnt food — if the cover itself is burnt,
  pitted, or the cavity paint is damaged, stop use and book an engineer.
- **Smelly or smoky after food spill**: clean the cavity with warm soapy water and heat a bowl
  of water with lemon juice for 3 minutes to loosen residue.
- **Buttons unresponsive**: check child lock (hold Stop for 3 seconds) and do the 60-second
  unplug reset.

## DO NOT — Specialist Only (NEVER instruct the customer to do these)
- **NEVER open the outer cover/case for any reason**: the high-voltage capacitor stores a
  lethal charge long after unplugging.
- **NEVER attempt to discharge the high-voltage capacitor** — no exceptions, regardless of
  guides found online.
- **Magnetron, transformer, HV diode, or capacitor replacement**.
- **Door interlock switch testing, adjustment, replacement, or bypass**: never operate the
  oven with the door open or tamper with the latch.
- **Waveguide cover replacement that requires tools inside the cavity wall**.
- **Fuse replacement inside the unit** (the internal fuse is behind the cover, near HV parts).
- **Operating the oven empty for test purposes longer than stated above**.
- **Any repair to a unit with a damaged or warped door, hinge, or seal** (microwave leakage risk).
