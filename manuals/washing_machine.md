# AquaSpin WM-700 Front-Loading Washing Machine

## Product Overview
Freestanding 8 kg front-loading washing machine. Mains powered (230 V), cold-water fill,
front-accessible drain pump filter behind the kick-panel flap. Detergent drawer top-left.
Child lock and digital fault codes (E-prefixed) on the display.

## Safe Troubleshooting — steps you MAY guide the customer through
- **Won't start**: check the door is firmly shut (listen for the latch click), the child lock
  isn't active (padlock icon — hold Start for 3 seconds to clear), and the socket works by
  plugging in another small appliance.
- **Won't drain / E02 code**: turn off and unplug the machine, open the kick-panel flap at the
  bottom front, place a shallow tray and towel, slowly unscrew the drain pump filter cap,
  remove lint or coins, screw it back hand-tight.
- **Won't fill / E01 code**: check the water tap behind the machine is fully open and the inlet
  hose isn't kinked.
- **Bad smell**: run an empty 90 °C maintenance wash with a machine cleaner or a cup of white
  vinegar; wipe the door seal fold dry and leave the door ajar between washes.
- **Excessive vibration**: check the machine is level (adjust the front feet by hand), confirm
  all shipping/transit bolts were removed from the rear panel, and avoid washing single heavy
  items like one bath mat alone.
- **Detergent not dispensing**: pull the detergent drawer fully out (press the release tab) and
  rinse it under warm water.
- **Door won't open after cycle**: wait 2–3 minutes after the cycle ends for the thermal door
  lock to release; if water is visible in the drum, run the Drain/Spin program first.

## DO NOT — Specialist Only (NEVER instruct the customer to do these)
- **Mains wiring / plug or socket repairs**: never open the plug, rewire, or bypass the fuse.
- **Drum bearings**: never attempt to replace or lubricate drum bearings (requires full cabinet
  disassembly and drum splitting).
- **Heater element or thermostat replacement**: live mains components inside the cabinet.
- **Door interlock replacement or bypass**: never defeat or bridge the door lock switch.
- **Removing the top, side, or rear panels**: exposes mains wiring and the capacitor-start motor.
- **Motor, control board, or inverter repairs**.
- **Emergency door release when the drum is hot or full of water above the door line**.
