# BaristaPro EX-9 Semi-Automatic Espresso Machine

## Product Overview
Counter-top semi-automatic espresso machine with a 15-bar vibration pump, thermoblock heater,
58 mm portafilter, removable 2 L water tank, steam wand, and a user-run descale program.
Mains powered (230 V). Pressurised boiler circuit inside the sealed chassis.

## Safe Troubleshooting — steps you MAY guide the customer through
- **No power**: check the socket with another appliance and make sure the rear rocker switch
  (not just the front button) is on.
- **No water flow / sputtering**: refill the tank and reseat it firmly (the valve underneath must
  engage), then run a blank shot (no coffee) to re-prime the pump.
- **Weak or fast-flowing coffee**: use a finer grind, dose 18 g for a double, and tamp evenly;
  confirm the portafilter is locked fully to the right.
- **Slow drips / no flow with coffee**: the grind may be too fine or the basket holes blocked —
  soak the (cooled) basket in warm soapy water and clear holes with the supplied cleaning pin.
- **"Descale" light on**: run the built-in descale program exactly as follows: empty tank, add
  descaler solution, hold the 2-cup button 5 seconds, let the full program complete, then run
  two tanks of fresh water through.
- **Steam wand blocked**: let the machine cool fully, wipe the wand, and clear the tip hole with
  the cleaning pin; purge briefly after every milk steam.
- **Leaking from the group head**: remove (when cool) and rinse the group gasket area and the
  shower screen if hand-removable; re-lock the portafilter firmly.

## DO NOT — Specialist Only (NEVER instruct the customer to do these)
- **Opening the chassis or any screwed panel**: exposes mains wiring and the pressurised circuit.
- **Thermoblock / boiler work**: never open, descale manually, or repair the boiler or
  thermoblock — it is a sealed pressure component.
- **Over-pressure valve (OPV) adjustment or replacement**: pressure vessel safety device.
- **Pump replacement or internal hose/clamp work**.
- **Thermal fuse or heating element replacement**.
- **Bypassing the steam wand safety valve or operating with a damaged wand**.
- **Any repair while the machine is plugged in or still hot/pressurised**.
